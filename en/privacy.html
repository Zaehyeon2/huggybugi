Privacy Policy

This is a translation for reference. If anything differs, the Korean version applies.

In short

  • You sign up with an email and a password. We do not ask for your name, phone number or address.
  • Messages, photos and doodles are stored locked and deleted after 24 hours.
  • Nothing is sold or handed to another company. The servers run on Cloudflare.
  • Delete your account and it goes right away (up to 30 days later in backups).
  • If someone reports a message, the operator sees it and up to 10 messages before it. Report records are deleted after a year.
  • You must be 14 or older to sign up.
  • Questions are welcome at support@hammerturtle.xyz.

Hammer Turtle Lab (representative Kim Jaehyeon) (the "operator") processes the personal data of users of Huggybugi (the app, the web chat and this website, the "service") under the Korean Personal Information Protection Act, and sets out this privacy policy.

Article 4 (How data is destroyed)

  • The server deletes data past its retention period automatically every hour. Deleting your account deletes it right away.
  • Electronic files are deleted so they cannot be recovered. Database backups keep them for up to 30 days, after which they disappear automatically.
  • No personal data is handled on paper.

The operator does not share personal data with third parties, except where a statute specifically requires it.

Article 8 (Your rights, duties and how to use them)

  • You can ask to see, correct, delete or transfer your personal data, ask that processing stop, or withdraw consent, at any time.
  • Your nickname, status text and uploaded images can be changed or deleted in the app, and you can delete your account under Settings > Account.
  • For anything else, write to the contact in Article 12 and you will hear back within 10 days. You may also act through a representative.
  • If you ask for a transfer, your personal data is sent to you by email as a JSON file.
  • You must look after your own account and password, and must not upload or infringe anyone else's personal data.

Article 9 (Children under 14)

Children under 14 cannot sign up, and age is confirmed at sign-up. If the operator learns that data from a child under 14 has been processed, it is deleted immediately.

Article 10 (Security measures)

  • Passwords are stored in a form that cannot be reversed (one-way hashing).
  • All traffic is encrypted with https, and messages and photos are encrypted again with the group key.
  • Sign-in and sign-up attempts are rate limited.
  • Only one person, the operator, can reach the servers and the database.

Article 11 (Automatic collection)

No cookies are used. The web chat keeps your session and display settings in browser storage (localStorage), which goes away when you sign out or clear site data in your browser. No advertising or behavioral analytics tools are used.
