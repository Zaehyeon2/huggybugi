Check before you download

            
    
              
  • Are the actions right? The AI may have changed the order. Tap a picture to move it to the right action (on a PC you can drag), or pick an action from the list if a whole row is off.
    • 
              
  • Does it face right? It should face front or right. If it faces left, press that row's Flip.
    • 
              
  • Is the size OK? Compare with Ddeoggi on each row and adjust with the slider. A body that gets cut off is outlined in red.
