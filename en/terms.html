Article 9 (Paid features)

Products (extra slots, group member spots, Slot a Lot, character effects, decor slots, FX Flex) and prices (VAT included) are shown on the payment page and paid once. No product is charged again automatically. Prices may change, but a change does not affect products you have already paid for. Except for FX Flex, products do not expire. They are added to your account (group member spots to the group you chose) as soon as payment is confirmed.

Group member spots attach to the group you choose and raise how many people can join it. Any member of that group can buy them, and a group can be raised to at most 30 people. If the group is deleted, the purchased spots disappear with it.

Slot a Lot is paid once and, with no time limit, lets you use characters, items, emotes, bubble colors and groups with no count limit, including upload types added in the future, and raises your decor slots to 5. It does not include character effects, group member spots or other products sold separately.

Character effects are effects worn by your desktop character. Each one is bought separately and can stay on with no time limit. If an effect is taken off sale, people who already bought it can keep turning it on.

Decor slots raise how many decorations you can turn on at once. You can buy slots up to a total of 5, counting the 1 free slot. If you have Slot a Lot, you already have 5 and cannot buy more.

FX Flex is a time-limited pass that, for 1 month (30 days), 3 months (90 days) or 1 year (365 days), lets you turn on every character effect on sale (including FX Flex exclusive effects) and use 1 extra decor slot.

  • The period starts when payment is confirmed. If you still have FX Flex time left (including the one-day trial), the new period is added to the end of it.
  • It is not charged automatically, so it ends when the period is over without you having to cancel. To stop during the period, request a refund under the refund rules below.
  • When the period ends, effects that only FX Flex let you turn on are turned off. Character effects you bought stay.
  • An account that has never had FX Flex can try it free once, for one day.

Products are digital entitlements, so nothing is shipped and they cannot be exchanged for another product. If you need a different one, you can get a refund and buy again.

Within 7 days of payment, you can get a full refund by writing to support@hammerturtle.xyz. If one order contains several products, each product is refunded separately. The following cases are not refundable.

  • Extra slots: you added that type after paying (for groups, created or joined one). Deleting it afterwards does not make it refundable.
  • Group member spots: anyone joined that group after the spots were added
  • Slot a Lot: you added something beyond the free limit
  • Character effects: you turned the bought effect on even once. If you buy an effect you already had on through the one-day trial or FX Flex, it counts as turned on at the time of payment.
  • Decor slots: you used a slot you bought. If you have more decorations on than your free slots plus the slots added by FX Flex, the excess counts as using your bought slots, starting with the earliest bought.

FX Flex is fully refunded within 7 days of payment if you have not turned on any effect that only FX Flex lets you turn on (effects you do not own, FX Flex exclusive effects). Otherwise, 3-month and 1-year passes are refunded minus KRW 2,900 for each month used, and 1-month passes are not refunded. Months used are counted in 30-day units from when that pass period started, and a month that has started counts as fully used. Using the 1 extra decor slot from FX Flex does not count as using FX Flex.

Character effects, decor slots and FX Flex are digital content you can use as soon as payment is confirmed. Before payment, the payment page tells you when refunds are limited, and before you buy, we offer a preview that puts the effect on your character and the FX Flex one-day trial.

Products that could not be added to your account after payment (an effect you already own, slots or member spots over the limit, and so on) are canceled for that amount right away. If the cancellation does not happen automatically, the operator refunds it.

Before payment, closing the payment window cancels it. After payment, cancellation follows the refund rules above.

Refunds go back to the payment method you used and may take 3 to 7 business days depending on your card issuer.
