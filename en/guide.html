Make with an image AI

  1. Copy the prompt from Make from one picture.
  2. Paste it into an image AI like ChatGPT or Gemini. Add a photo of your character too, if you have one.
  3. Approve the base look, then pick actions or say "You decide".
  4. Upload the picture you got to that page and it gets cut per action.
  5. Compare with Ddeoggi for size, tidy up, and download the zip.
  6. Drag the zip into the app Create window.

You can upload once you have idle, walk and sleep.

Draw it yourself

One PNG per action, with the frames laid out side by side.

Specs

  • 96×48 character frames, 48×48 item frames, up to 8 frames per action (4 character frames make 384×48)
  • Draw characters in the middle of the 96 cell. Wide characters can use the whole width
  • Transparent background, 200KB or less per file (the app re-saves bigger ones to shrink them)
  • Sound effect wav: 50KB or less
  • Facing front with the face turned slightly right, or facing right (the app flips it for left)
  • Character and poop touch the bottom row, centered horizontally
  • Throwables and food go in the center of the frame
  • Character size: 800 to 1200 painted pixels, 30 to 46 pixels tall (bigger if the body is thin)
  • The app doesn't scale up, so a small drawing looks tiny next to friends' characters

Poop

  • One fly picture is enough. Draw it as it lies on the floor, with no rotation
  • The app doesn't shrink it and draws the full 48×48 cells
  • Draw it small at the bottom center of the frame and leave the top empty
  • The default poop is 17 wide and 12 tall
  • It doesn't have to be poop. A mini pumpkin, a coffin, or a ball of yarn works too
  • Keep it inside the bottom center 24×24

Templates

  • Get a template with an example for each action from "Get template" in the Create window (the app hamster, an acorn for items)
  • Blank templates: 2 frames, 4 frames, 8 frames, Download all (zip)
  • Draw on a layer below it, then turn off the template layer and save
  • The red line is the foot baseline
  • Drawing tools: Piskel(web, free), LibreSprite(free), Aseprite(paid)

Check before uploading

  • 48 tall, width a multiple of 96 for characters or 48 for items, 8 frames or fewer
  • Transparent background, no template lines left
  • Not all four corners of a frame are filled (if they are, it is treated as a leftover background and rejected)
  • Facing right, feet on the bottom row, same position in every frame

Uploading

  1. In the tray menu "Character > Create…", pick the Characters, Items, or Emotes tab.
  2. Press "+ New" and enter a name (up to 20 characters).
  3. Pick a PNG for each action, or drag one onto its slot.
  4. Save it and it shows up in your list. Edit or delete it from the same place.
  • The recommended fps is already filled in
  • For items, also pick the kind (throwable, food, poop) and a sound effect wav
  • You can also open it with the + card at the start of the change character, item, and emote windows
  • The character you pick shows right away
  • On friends' screens it shows up when they open the tray "Character > Change…" window or click "Get new characters and items"

Using food and poop

  • Food: right-click a friend, pick one under "Food", then click the friend
  • Every click feeds them until you pick "Throwable" again
  • Poop: the fly picture is drawn as poop lying on the floor (it isn't thrown or used as food)
  • Your character poops the one you picked when it's full
  • Poop a friend hasn't received yet shows as the default poop until they get it

Emotes

  • In the "Emotes" tab, pick a GIF, WebP, APNG, PNG, or JPG
  • The app shrinks it to 64px and saves it at its original speed
  • Only friends in the same group see it

Details
  • Pick up to 16 frames and save
  • Original up to 20MB, up to 2048px wide and tall
  • Names go up to 20 characters and can't repeat within a group
  • You can't use : or spaces, because they break :name: in a message
  • You also can't use default emote names (heart, question, exclamation, angry, lol, note, 쪽지)

Add them all at once

  • Zip your PNGs into one file and drag it onto the Create window
  • You can also pick a zip file or folder from "Add in bulk"
  • It shows which slot each file goes to before adding
  • For files whose names don't match, pick the slot yourself
  • The "Download all" zip above uses these names too, so you can try adding it as is

File names are exactly the action names (case doesn't matter, folders inside the zip are fine).

  • Characters: idle.png walk.png sleep.png poked.png throw.png hit.png jump.png eat.png wave.png happy.png offline.png
  • Items: fly.png impact.png, sound sound.wav (just fly.png for poop)

Make with the Claude or Codex skill

  • Install the Claude Code or Codex plugin from a terminal (paste the commands below)
  • Send a photo and say "Make me a Huggybugi character"
  • It draws 11 actions (poop, throwable, and food too if you want) and gives you a zip to add as in "Add them all at once" above
  • New versions come along with plugin updates
