Pay timer

Today's earnings climb second by second. Show them above your character and hide the amount with a shortcut. Your salary stays on this device.

Focus

Focus timer

15, 25 or 50 minutes, or pick an end time, and your friends' characters stay hidden until then. Friends see 'In the zone', and throwing at you gets a 'Shh!'.

Status

FAQ

Is it really free?

Free to use. There is a cap on how many characters and items you can upload.

Can anyone read it if I use my work computer?

Messages and photos are locked before they leave your computer, so a network in between cannot read them. They are deleted after 24 hours.

Does it work on a phone?

On a phone you join the same room through the web. The character play happens in the desktop app.

Can I make my own character?

Yes. Upload your own drawings or ask an AI. Have a look at the how to make one page.

I want to check the file I downloaded is the real one

Compare it with the SHA-256 shown under the download. On Windows run Get-FileHash <path> in PowerShell, on Mac run shasum -a 256 <path> in Terminal.

It says "unidentified developer" the first time I open it

On Mac go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and choose "Open Anyway". On Windows choose "More info" > "Run anyway".
